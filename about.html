01 / Yente Vanhoudt

About me.

I'm Yente, a Digital Experience Design student exploring web design, 3D and UI/UX. I enjoy turning ideas into thoughtful digital experiences, with an eye for detail.

I'm an outgoing young guy who's always open to learning something new. I like trying things out, following an idea and seeing where it takes me.

Experimenting is how I learn. Whether something works straight away or takes a few attempts, I want to keep improving and discovering what I can make next.

In my free time, I enjoy going to the gym and riding my motorcycle. I like making time for both, alongside my creative work.
